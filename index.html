Fold Your Name

A full-stack bioinformatics demo: converts any name into an amino-acid sequence, calls the ESMFold structure-prediction API, and renders the resulting 3D protein live in the browser with per-residue confidence coloring. Built for a career fair to pair with an NFC business card and a live scrolling hardware display.

Diabetes Risk Prediction

Trained a Random Forest classifier on the Pima Indians Diabetes dataset to predict diabetes risk from clinical measurements. Cleaned biologically implausible zero-values via median imputation, then evaluated feature importance — glucose, BMI, and age emerged as the strongest predictors, consistent with established clinical knowledge. 74.7% test accuracy.

Python · scikit-learn · pandas · seaborn
